# Pricing - Missouri Aetna Plans 2026

## ⚠️ Important Note

This page shows pricing information for **Aetna Medicare Premier (H2663-021)** as an example.

**Actual pricing varies significantly by plan code.** Each of the 34 plans may have:
- Different monthly premiums ($0 to $40)
- Different deductibles
- Different copays for services
- Different maximum out-of-pocket (MOOP) amounts
- Different prescription drug costs
- Different benefit limits (dental, vision, hearing, etc.)

**Always verify pricing for your specific plan code in the Evidence of Coverage (EOC) document or call Aetna at 1-833-570-6670.**

## Out-of-Pocket Costs by Service Type

### Example: Aetna Medicare Premier (H2663-021)

**Plan Deductible**: $0 (no deductible)

**Maximum Out-of-Pocket (MOOP)**: $2,900 for in-network services

**What This Means**:
- Once you pay $2,900 in copays and coinsurance for covered medical services in a year, the plan pays 100% of your remaining covered medical services
- Your premium and prescription drug costs do NOT count toward your MOOP

## Cost-Saving Tips

1. **Use In-Network Providers**: Lower copays and better rates
2. **Generic Drugs First**: Tier 1 and 2 drugs have lower costs
3. **Mail-Order Pharmacy**: Some plans offer lower copays for 90-day supplies
4. **Preventive Services**: Many services covered at $0
5. **Reach MOOP**: Once you hit $2,900 out-of-pocket (H2663-021 example), plan covers remaining costs 100%
6. **Select PCP Wisely**: Some services are free when PCP provides them

---

## What Counts Toward Your Out-of-Pocket Maximum?

**Counts**:
- Copays for medical services
- Coinsurance for medical services

**Does NOT Count**:
- Monthly premiums
- Part D prescription drug costs
- Medicare Part B premium
- Extra Benefits Card spending

---

## Important Premium Notes

- **Part B Premium**: Separate and additional; you must pay this to be in any Medicare plan
- **Plan Premium Due**: Usually deducted from Social Security or paid directly by you
- **Zero-Premium Plans**: Available but still require Medicare Part B payment
- **Price Changes**: Premiums and copays can change yearly
