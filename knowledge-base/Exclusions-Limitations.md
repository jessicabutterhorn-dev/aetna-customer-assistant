## Services NOT Covered

### Cosmetic & Non-Medical Procedures
- ❌ Cosmetic surgery (unless medically necessary, e.g., after injury or reconstruction)
- ❌ Cosmetic dentistry
- ❌ Laser eye surgery for vision correction (LASIK)
- ❌ Routine wart or mole removal for cosmetic reasons

### Non-Medicare Covered Services
- ❌ Care not covered by Original Medicare (with some plan exceptions)
- ❌ Non-FDA approved treatments or experimental procedures
- ❌ Treatments available through Medicaid (not covered under Medicare Advantage)

### International Care (Generally NOT Covered)
- ❌ Routine care received outside the United States
- ⚠️ **Emergency care outside U.S. IS covered** (max $250,000)
- ❌ Non-emergency care while traveling
- ❌ Planned elective procedures outside U.S.

### Transportation
- ❌ Routine, non-emergency transportation
- ❌ Taxi or Uber to medical appointments
- ✓ **Emergency ambulance IS covered**

### Self-Care & Home Supplies
- ❌ Medications you purchase over-the-counter without prescription
- ❌ Vitamins (unless medically necessary and prescribed)
- ❌ Health/fitness club memberships
- ❌ Self-help programs (except those covered as mental health services)

### Hearing & Vision Limitations
- ❌ Hearing aids replacement beyond annual benefit limit ($1,250/ear/year)
- ❌ Eyeglass/contact lens replacement beyond annual benefit limit ($475/year)
- ❌ Routine eye exams exceed annual limit (1/year covered)

### Mental Health/Substance Abuse Treatment
- ⚠️ Inpatient psychiatric hospitalization: Limited to 190 days per benefit year
- ❌ Some residential treatment facilities not covered
- ✓ Outpatient services ARE covered (with copay)

### Dental Limitations
- ❌ Services exceeding annual benefit ($4,000/year)
- ❌ Orthdontics (braces) — not covered
- ❌ Cosmetic dentistry
- ❌ Implants — check specific plan (not all cover these)
- ❌ Certain periodontal treatments (varies by plan)

## Visit Limits & Restrictions

### Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF)
- **Limit**: Up to 100 days per benefit year
- **Requirement**: Must meet CMS criteria for medically necessary skilled care
- **Restrictions**: Can't exceed 100 days; hospital stay doesn't need to precede SNF

### Psychiatric Hospital (Inpatient)
- **Limit**: Up to 190 days per benefit year
- **Requirement**: Medical necessity for inpatient psychiatric care
- **Restrictions**: Preauthorization required

### Physical/Occupational/Speech Therapy
- ⚠️ **May have visit limits** — varies by specific plan
- **Requirement**: Medical necessity
- **Restriction**: Prior authorization often required

### Chiropractic Care
- ⚠️ **Limited or not covered** — check specific plan EOC
- **Typically NOT covered** under Medicare Advantage

## Prior Authorization Requirements

### Services Requiring Pre-Approval
These require Aetna approval BEFORE you get them (your doctor usually submits):

**Often Require Prior Auth**:
- Inpatient hospital admissions
- Certain surgeries
- High-cost imaging (MRI, CT scans)
- Specialty referrals
- SNF stays
- Mental health inpatient care
- Some specialty medications
- Certain physical therapy services

**Important**: Without pre-approval, you may face:
- Denial of coverage
- Requirement to pay full cost out-of-pocket
- Delayed treatment while approval is pending

**Action**: Your doctor's office should obtain prior authorization — confirm this is done before your appointment

## Prescription Drug Limitations

### Deductible Limitations
- **Only applies to Tiers 3, 4, 5**
- **Does NOT apply to**: Tiers 1, 2, insulins, vaccines
- **Deductible**: $615 (2026)

### Tier 5 (Specialty) Drugs
- ⚠️ **No long-term supply available** (90-day refill not available)
- **Must fill monthly** at pharmacy
- Higher copayment (25% coinsurance, typically)
- **Requires prior authorization** frequently

### Quantity Limits
- ⚠️ Certain drugs have quantity restrictions per prescription
- **Example**: Some pain medications limited to certain daily dosage
- **Requires prior authorization** for quantities exceeding limits
- Your pharmacist will inform you if limits apply

### Prior Authorization for Drugs
- ⚠️ **Many medications require pre-approval** before plan covers them
- **Examples**: Some biologics, brand-name drugs, newer medications
- **Timeline**: Usually 24-72 hours for approval
- **Non-compliance**: If not pre-approved, you may pay full cost

### Step Therapy
- Some plans require you to TRY a cheaper drug first
- Only covers more expensive medication if cheaper one doesn't work
- **Requires documentation** that you've tried step 1 drug
- Can appeal if medically necessary

## Medical Service Limitations

### Emergency Care (Outside U.S.)
- **Worldwide coverage limit**: $250,000 maximum
- **Once limit reached**: No more emergency coverage outside U.S.
- ⚠️ **Plan pays**: Limited to what Medicare would pay for similar U.S. care

### Out-of-Network Care
- ⚠️ May NOT be covered (HMO plans specifically)
- **Exception**: Emergency care (partially covered)
- **May require full payment** if you use out-of-network provider
- You'll need to submit for reimbursement (not guaranteed)

### Preventive Services
- **Limitation**: May not cover additional preventive tests beyond Medicare coverage
- **Example**: Extensive cancer screening beyond standard recommendations not covered
- **Check EOC** for full list of covered preventive services

## Behavioral Health Limitations

### Substance Abuse Treatment
- ⚠️ Limited to what Medicare covers
- **Inpatient**: Limited number of days
- **Outpatient**: Usually covered with copay
- ⚠️ **Not all facilities in-network**; may require prior auth

### Mental Health Limitations
- ⚠️ Inpatient psychiatric: Limited to 190 days/year
- ⚠️ Some intensive outpatient programs (IOP) may not be covered
- **Requirement**: Medical necessity for all mental health services

## Waiting Periods & Special Rules

### Dental Waiting Periods
- ⚠️ **Major dental services**: 12-month waiting period may apply
- **Preventive services**: Usually no waiting period
- **Prior coverage**: May waive waiting period if you had dental coverage

### Vision Waiting Periods
- Usually **NO waiting period** for vision services
- Benefit can be used immediately upon enrollment

### Prescription Drug
- ⚠️ **Generic drugs**: Covered immediately
- ⚠️ **Brand-name drugs**: May have step-therapy requirement
- **Insulin exception**: Special pricing applies immediately

## Coverage Exclusions by Category

### NOT Covered in ANY Plans
- Personal convenience items (at-home safety rails, etc.)
- Self-care management programs (unless specific plan covers)
- Weight loss programs (unless medically necessary)
- Foot care (routine) — unless diabetic
- Hearing aids beyond annual benefit amount
- Eyewear beyond annual benefit amount
- Acupuncture (beyond 20 visits/year in some plans)

### Covered with Limitations
- Hearing aids: Annual $1,250/ear benefit limit
- Eyewear: Annual $475 benefit limit
- Dental: Annual $4,000 benefit limit
- SNF: 100 days/year maximum
- Psychiatric hospitalization: 190 days/year maximum

## What to Do About Exclusions

### Before Receiving Care
1. ✓ Check your **Evidence of Coverage (EOC)**
2. ✓ Call Aetna: **1-833-570-6670** (member services)
3. ✓ Ask specifically: "Is [specific service] covered under my plan?"
4. ✓ Request **prior authorization** if required

### If Service Is Denied
1. ✓ Request denial in writing
2. ✓ Appeal with medical documentation
3. ✓ Contact **Patient Advocate Foundation** for help
4. ✓ File appeal within 60 days of denial notice

### Questions?
- **Aetna Member Services**: 1-833-570-6670 (8 AM-8 PM, 7 days/week)
- **TTY**: 711
- **Online**: AetnaMedicare.com/[your plan code]
- **Request**: Complete Evidence of Coverage (EOC)
