# Eligibility - Missouri Aetna Plans

## ⚠️ Important Note About Service Areas

**Each of the 34 plans serves different Missouri counties.** Before enrolling:
1. **Find your county** (where you live)
2. **Confirm at least one plan code covers your county**
3. **Check which specific plan codes serve your county**

Aetna has 34 plans because they cover different combinations of Missouri counties. Your residence determines which plans are available to you.

---

## Who Can Enroll?

To enroll in any Aetna Medicare plan in Missouri, you must:

1. ✓ **Be entitled to Medicare Part A**
   - Typically available at age 65
   - May be available earlier if you have ESRD (End-Stage Renal Disease) or ALS

2. ✓ **Have Medicare Part B**
   - Must be currently enrolled
   - Will continue paying Part B premium

3. ✓ **Live in the plan's service area**
   - Different plans cover different Missouri counties
   - See "Service Areas by Plan" below

4. ✓ **Be a U.S. citizen or lawful resident**
   - Verified before enrollment

5. ✓ **Not be incarcerated**

---

## Service Area by Plan Code

There are **34 different Aetna Medicare plans** in Missouri, each serving different counties.

**To find which plans serve your county:**

1. **Visit**: Medicare.gov/plan-compare
2. **Enter your zip code** or county name
3. **See all available plans** for your area
4. **Select the plan code** you want information about

**Example Service Area**:
- **Aetna Medicare Premier (H2663-021)** covers: Barry, Barton, Cedar, Christian, Dade, Dallas, Douglas, Greene, Hickory, Jasper, Laclede, Lawrence, McDonald, Newton, Ozark, Polk, Stone, Taney, Webster, Wright counties
- **Other plans** (H2663-005, H2663-026, H2663-041, H2663-063, etc.) each cover different county combinations

**Important**: Different plan codes cover different areas, so you must verify your specific county is covered BEFORE enrolling.

## Enrollment Periods

### Open Enrollment
- **When**: October 15 - December 7 (yearly)
- **Effective Date**: January 1 following enrollment period
- **Available To**: All Medicare-eligible individuals

### Special Enrollment Periods (SEP)
Available if you have a qualifying life event:

**Qualifying Life Events**:
- Loss of health insurance (job loss, COBRA expiration)
- Death of spouse
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Marriage or divorce
- Relocation outside plan's service area
- Certain changes in income/Medicaid status
- Permanent move to long-term care facility
- Loss of coverage due to plan termination
- Turning 65 (first 3 months)
- Medicaid eligibility changes

**Action**: Contact Aetna within 60 days of qualifying event

### Initial Enrollment Period (IEP)
- **When**: 3 months before to 3 months after month you turn 65
- **Available To**: First-time Medicare enrollees turning 65

## Who Should NOT Enroll?

❌ **Do NOT enroll if**:

- You don't meet the basic Medicare requirements
- Your county is not in the plan's service area
- You have End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) requiring dialysis — special rules apply
- You have ALS and use certain disability benefits — check with Aetna
- You're currently incarcerated
- You're not a U.S. citizen or lawful resident
- You want to keep Original Medicare only (but you can have both)

## How to Enroll

### Option 1: Online
1. Visit **Medicare.gov**
2. Select your plan
3. Follow enrollment steps
4. Effective date: January 1 following enrollment

### Option 2: Phone
- **Call**: 1-833-859-6031 (TTY: 711)
- **Hours**: Oct 1-Mar 31: 8 AM-8 PM, 7 days/week
- **Hours**: Apr 1-Sep 30: 8 AM-8 PM, Mon-Fri
- Aetna will confirm eligibility and enroll you

### Option 3: In Person
- Contact your local Social Security office
- Community senior centers may offer enrollment assistance

### Option 4: Agent/Broker
- Licensed insurance agent can assist with enrollment
- No additional cost to you

## What to Have Ready for Enrollment

- Medicare card
- Social Security number
- Proof of residency (recent utility bill, lease, or driver's license)
- List of current medications (for drug coverage review)
- List of preferred doctors/hospitals
- Insurance information

---

## After Enrollment

### Before Your Coverage Starts
- You'll receive your member ID card (usually 1-2 weeks before effective date)
- You'll receive your formulary (drug list)
- Enrollment confirmation will be mailed
- Plan materials will be mailed

### When Coverage Begins (January 1)
- Your new plan is active
- Use your member ID card at doctor visits and pharmacies
- Original Medicare ends
- You can call Aetna at 1-833-570-6670 with questions

### Important Changes to Report
- Change of address
- Change of doctors
- Change in income/Medicaid status
- Birth/death in household
- Loss of other insurance

## Health Status & Enrollment

**Important**: Most Aetna Medicare plans **DO NOT** have health questionnaires or medical underwriting. This means:
- ✓ You cannot be denied enrollment due to pre-existing conditions
- ✓ No health screening required
- ✓ You can enroll regardless of current health status
- ✓ Pre-existing conditions are covered immediately

**Exception**: Some specific plans may have restrictions — contact Aetna to confirm

---

## Disenrollment (Leaving a Plan)

You can leave an Aetna plan during:
- **Open Enrollment**: Oct 15 - Dec 7
- **Special Enrollment Periods**: 60 days from qualifying event
- **Coverage Takes Effect**: January 1 or first of month following disenrollment request

**New Coverage Options**:
- Switch to different Aetna plan
- Switch to Original Medicare
- Switch to another Medicare Advantage plan
- Choose Medigap (supplemental coverage)

---

## Dual Eligible Individuals (Medicare + Medicaid)

If you have both Medicare and Medicaid:
- ✓ You can enroll in Aetna Medicare plans
- ✓ Medicaid may cover plan costs not covered by Medicare
- ✓ You may need to coordinate benefits
- **Action**: Contact your local Medicaid office and Aetna for coordination

---

## Special Populations

### Retirees from Previous Employers
- You may enroll in Aetna if you meet basic Medicare requirements
- Group coverage through employer may affect enrollment timing
- **Action**: Contact your former employer benefits department

### Individuals with ESRD (Kidney Dialysis)
- Special rules apply (36-month coordination of benefits period)
- Not all plans available to all ESRD members
- **Action**: Contact Aetna directly to discuss eligibility

### Individuals with ALS (Lou Gehrig's Disease)
- Some coverage restrictions may apply
- Enroll during your IEP or current SEP
- **Action**: Contact Aetna to confirm eligibility

## Important Reminder

**You must have both Medicare Part A AND Part B to enroll in any Medicare Advantage plan.** If you only have Part A, you'll need to enroll in Part B first (may have late enrollment penalties if you wait).

For help with Medicare Part B enrollment, contact Medicare.gov or call 1-800-MEDICARE.
