## D-SNP Plans (Dual Eligible Special Needs Plans)

> D-SNP plans require both Medicare AND Medicaid eligibility. All D-SNP plans have $0 monthly premium, $0 deductible, and $0 copays for Medicare-covered services (Medicaid covers cost sharing). MOOP is $9,250/year but effectively $0 so long as Medicaid covers cost sharing.

## Notes

- **D-SNP plans** (H5325 series) require both Medicare AND Medicaid eligibility. The specific Medicaid category (QMB, QMB+, SLMB+, FBDE) determines eligibility for each D-SNP plan. "Full Dual" plans (H5325-012 through H5325-015) require full Medicaid benefits (QMB+, SLMB+, or FBDE) — QMB-only members do not qualify.
- **C-SNP plans** (H2663-098, H2663-102) require a qualifying chronic condition (diabetes mellitus, chronic heart failure, and/or cardiovascular disorders).
- **H2663-022** is unique: it has NO Part D prescription drug coverage. Members must enroll in a standalone Part D plan (PDP) separately.
- **H1608-050** is unique: it has a $1,500 plan deductible that applies before most cost sharing begins.
- **H2663-022** provides a $150/month Part B premium reduction through Social Security.
- All plans also cover **out-of-state emergencies** and **urgent care nationwide**.
- OTC allowances and Medical Expense Wallets are loaded onto the Aetna Medicare Extra Benefits Card (CVS-based).
