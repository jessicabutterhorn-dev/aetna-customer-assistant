# Coverage Details - Missouri Aetna Plans

## ⚠️ Important Note

This page shows **example coverage details from Aetna Medicare Premier (H2663-021)**.

- Different copay amounts
- Different coverage limits

- Different formularies (drug lists)

**Always verify coverage for your specific plan code in the Evidence of Coverage (EOC) document or call Aetna at 1-833-570-6670.**

## Medicare Part D Prescription Drugs

**Formulary**: B2 (2026)

### Deductible Phase
- Deductible Limit: $615
- Applies to: Tiers 3, 4, and 5 drugs

### Initial Coverage Phase (30-day supply at retail)

| Tier | Preferred Retail | Standard Retail | Mail (30-day) |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Tier 1: Preferred Generic | $0 | $2 | $0 |
| Tier 2: Generic | $0 | $12 | $0 |
| Tier 3: Preferred Brand | 24% coinsurance | 24% coinsurance | 24% coinsurance |
| Tier 4: Non-Preferred | 25% coinsurance | 25% coinsurance | 25% coinsurance |
| Tier 5: Specialty | 25% coinsurance | 25% coinsurance | 25% coinsurance |

### Insulin & Vaccines
- **Part D Insulins**: $35 maximum for one-month supply (any tier, any phase)
- **Vaccines**: Many covered at no cost, even before deductible

### Out-of-Pocket Threshold
- **Limit**: $2,100/year for Part D out-of-pocket costs

### Catastrophic Coverage Phase
- **Plan Pays**: 100% of covered Part D drugs
- **Your Cost**: $0 for generic and brand name drugs

## Extra Benefits (Available on Certain Plans)

## Important Coverage Notes

1. **Prior Authorization**: Some services require pre-approval before coverage
2. **Primary Care Provider**: You must select a PCP (one will be assigned if you don't choose)
3. **Network Providers**: Best rates when using in-network providers
4. **Referrals**: Not required to see specialists, but some providers may request recommendations
5. **Out-of-Network**: Coverage varies; may require full payment and reimbursement request
