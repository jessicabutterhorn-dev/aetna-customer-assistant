# Missouri Aetna Plans Overview

## 2026 Available Plans (34 Total)

### Plan Types Explained

- **HMO-POS**: Health Maintenance Organization with Point of Service option (limited out-of-network coverage)
- **HMO**: Health Maintenance Organization (mostly in-network only)
- **PPO**: Preferred Provider Organization (more flexibility with out-of-network)
- **D-SNP**: Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (for Medicare + Medicaid members)
- **C-SNP**: Chronic Care Special Needs Plan (for members with specific chronic conditions)

## Important: Plan Variations

Each plan code (e.g., H2663-005, H2663-026, etc.) serves **different Missouri counties** and may have:
- Different service areas
- Different copay amounts
- Different maximum out-of-pocket costs
- Different benefit amounts for dental/vision/hearing
- Different formularies (drug lists)

**Example**: H2663-005 and H2663-026 are both "Aetna Medicare Signature (HMO-POS)" but serve different counties with different benefits.

## Plan Selection Tips

- **Find Your County**: Determine which counties/plan codes serve your area
- **Zero-Premium Plans**: Most plans offer $0 premiums (except H2663-056 at $38.30, H2663-021 at $18, H2663-006 at $40)
- **Special Needs Plans**: D-SNP plans require Medicare + Medicaid; C-SNP plans for specific chronic conditions
- **Service Area**: Different plans cover different Missouri counties — **confirm your county is covered before enrolling**
- **Prescription Drug Coverage**: All plans include Medicare Part D drug coverage
- **Extra Benefits**: Most plans include dental, vision, and hearing coverage

## How to Get More Information

- **Full Details**: Visit AetnaMedicare.com with your plan code (e.g., AetnaMedicare.com/H2663-021)
- **Enrollment**: Call 1-833-859-6031 (Oct 1 - Mar 31: 8 AM-8 PM, 7 days/week; Apr 1 - Sep 30: 8 AM-8 PM, Mon-Fri)
- **Member Support**: Call 1-833-570-6670 (8 AM-8 PM, 7 days/week)
- **TTY**: 711
- **Evidence of Coverage**: Available online or by request — contains complete coverage details and limitations
